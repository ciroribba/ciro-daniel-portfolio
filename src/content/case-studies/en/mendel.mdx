## Expenses, cards, and invoicing

I implemented screens to configure spending limits, time periods, and permitted locations for card use. In the back office, I worked on processing receipts, assigning statuses, tracking invoicing requirements by country of purchase, and recording expenses for balances and metrics.

## Real-time status updates

We used Socket.IO to notify the back office when a receipt or payment ticket arrived for processing and validation. Updates from the back office let customers know whether their latest submission had passed validation, was pending, or had failed validation.
