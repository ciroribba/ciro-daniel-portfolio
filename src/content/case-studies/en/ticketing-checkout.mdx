## Rendering and purchase state

We used SSR for the homepage, event listings, and seat selection where applicable. The server delivered the initial view with prices, availability, and dates. In the browser, Zustand held the temporary selection and updated displayed prices and discounts.

After selecting tickets, users could sign in and continue to checkout. The server verified the session and confirmed availability, the final amount, cart expiration, and payment status.

## Checkout rules

- I worked on handling delays in payment provider confirmations. Each user could have one active cart, locked against editing and starting another purchase until the payment resolved or the cart expired.
- I implemented forms for club members, including discounts and zero-price tickets. I also contributed to the box-office flow, which followed its own rules.
- I mentored three junior developers and worked with GitHub Actions for CI/CD.
